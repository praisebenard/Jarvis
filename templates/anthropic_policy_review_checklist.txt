ANTHROPIC HACKERONE POLICY REVIEW CHECKLIST — MANUAL REVIEW ONLY

This checklist reflects policy details supplied by the user and may be
outdated. Verify the current official program rules before making decisions.
It does not authorize testing, confirm a vulnerability, or establish bounty
eligibility. JARVIS and AI-generated alerts remain unverified until manually
validated.

[ ] Is the exact asset, scheme, port, and path explicitly in active scope?
[ ] Are the testing methods permitted by the current program rules?
[ ] Is there a reproducible, manually validated security impact?
[ ] Are safe reproduction steps and evidence sufficient for another researcher?
[ ] Could the issue fall into a policy exclusion?
[ ] Is it a duplicate or potentially the same root cause as another report?
[ ] Can it be demonstrated without other users' accounts, private data, or disruption?
[ ] Does the report contain only the minimum evidence needed, with secrets redacted?

Potential exclusion warnings from the supplied policy summary:
- Denial of service, disruptive testing, brute force, and unauthenticated rate-limit testing.
- Social engineering, phishing, and attacks on accounts the researcher does not own.
- Clickjacking without a sensitive action; missing cookie flags alone.
- Generic TLS/security best-practice findings without working proof of impact.
- Model jailbreaks, harmful model outputs, and hallucinations under the technical program.
- Potential duplicate/root-cause overlap with the Claude Code permission-modal bypass remediation.

These are review warnings, not a substitute for checking the current official rules.
